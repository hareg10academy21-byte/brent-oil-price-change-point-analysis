# Assumptions and Limitations

## Assumptions

* The Brent oil price dataset is complete and accurately represents historical market prices.
* The event dates collected from historical records are approximately correct.
* Major geopolitical events, OPEC decisions, and economic crises may influence Brent oil prices.
* Bayesian change point models can identify statistically significant structural changes in the price series.
* Daily oil prices are sufficient to capture long-term market behavior.

## Limitations

* Oil prices are affected by many factors that may not be included in this study, including weather conditions, currency exchange rates, technological developments, and market speculation.
* Some events have delayed effects that may not appear immediately in the price data.
* Different events may overlap, making it difficult to isolate the impact of a single event.
* Bayesian models estimate probabilities rather than absolute certainty.

## Correlation vs. Causation

A change point occurring near the date of a historical event indicates a statistical association in time, but it does not prove that the event caused the price change. Other economic or political factors may have contributed simultaneously. Therefore, the results should be interpreted as evidence of possible relationships rather than definitive proof of causality.
